# Choose Reliable Orchestration Patterns: reduced-motion presentation

Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

## simplest-sufficient-pattern

Show the complete decision funnel as a static diagram.

Text alternative: The funnel asks whether one call works, whether control flow is known, and whether semantic routing is necessary before introducing an agent.

## deterministic-workflow-demonstration

Present all graph nodes, edges, and terminal states at once.

Text alternative: Intake leads to classification, optional specialist work, verification, one bounded revision, and explicit accept or escalate outcomes.

## pattern-selection-demonstration

Show the two complete designs side by side.

Text alternative: The open loop can choose and repeat any step. The governed graph requires intake, research, writing, two verification judgments, human approval, and publication.

## manager-and-handoff

Show both patterns side by side in a static comparison.

Text alternative: In delegation the manager remains active and receives a typed artifact. In handoff the specialist becomes active under a transfer contract.

## parallelism-and-joins

Show branch contracts and the complete join policy in one static view.

Text alternative: Each branch has isolated scope and budget. The join checks required results, timeout, conflict, cancellation, retry, and partial-failure policy.

## observe-and-limit

Display the contract and adapter matrix without animation.

Text alternative: Run, route, contract, budget, approval, retry, and terminal-state requirements stay constant across Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google implementation mappings.
