# Operate, Recover, and Improve Agent Systems: reduced-motion presentation

Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

## welcome-and-outcomes

Show the complete process diagram without animation.

Text alternative: The lesson follows seven steps from failure classification through verified improvement.

## classify-before-changing

Present the completed matrix as a static table.

Text alternative: The matrix lists model, prompt, tool, retrieval, data, policy, orchestration, and infrastructure, with the incident evidence assigned without claiming one root cause.

## triage-and-contain

Show the complete impact and containment table statically.

Text alternative: Users, data, permissions, external effects, money, availability, and legal or safety obligations determine severity and containment.

## incident-worked-example

Present the complete dossier without animated transitions.

Text alternative: Transfer-100 is confirmed, audit-7 is missing, notice-9 is unknown, and compensation restores operating 1000 and reserve 0.

## reconcile-and-recover

Show the entire recovery decision table statically.

Text alternative: The system of record, validated binding, postcondition, and retry budget determine the recovery action.

## unknown-outcome-demonstration

Show the simulated ledger and read-back as a static diagram.

Text alternative: The demonstration uses supplied evidence only and shows one confirmed message with no duplicate retry.

## learner-work-time

Keep the worksheet static during the silent work period.

Text alternative: Learners enter evidence and recovery actions for five possible outcomes.

## repair-lab

Show the complete lab instructions and output contract without animated terminal playback.

Text alternative: Learners edit only the reconciliation file, preserve fixtures, create a new output file, and inspect confirmed, compensated, missing, and pending entries.

## independent-variation

Show baseline and changed fixture values side by side.

Text alternative: The changed case requires 460 plus 40 to restore 500 and 40 minus 40 to restore 0, while retaining confirmed, missing, and unknown classifications.

## variation-prediction-pause

Keep the worksheet static during the silent pause.

Text alternative: Learners predict one confirmed action, one missing action, one unknown action, and the assertion requiring the missing write.

## runbook-and-communication

Show the full runbook and handoff packet as static ordered tables.

Text alternative: A responder can identify the incident, run safe local validation, find the unresolved notification, and identify the decision owner.

## learn-and-improve

Show the complete improvement loop in one static view.

Text alternative: Closure requires evidence, and prevention work has named owners, testable results, and a recurrence metric.

## ecosystem-transfer

Present the four-layer architecture without animation.

Text alternative: Retry guidance belongs to the failing interface, while postcondition reconciliation belongs to the consequential tool's system of record.

## answer-key-and-rubric

Show the rubric and causal chain without animation.

Text alternative: Credit requires evidence of safe generalization, not merely matching the expected terminal text.

## activity-work-time

Keep the activity checklist static.

Text alternative: Learners complete the operational evidence packet and may save or extend the activity.

## class-closing

Show the closing card without motion.

Text alternative: The lesson ends with classify, contain, reconcile, recover, communicate, and improve, while preserving uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.
