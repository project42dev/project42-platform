# AI Foundations Capstone: reduced-motion presentation

Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

## bounded-objective-explanation

Display every field simultaneously with numbered labels and no animated completion.

Text alternative: The brief names the user need, deliverable, acceptance checks, permitted data, authority, time box, and excluded scope.

## capstone-choice-demonstration

Present both objectives and comparison rows as a static table.

Text alternative: The rejected objective has production authority and unclear checks. The accepted objective uses synthetic evidence, no production action, explicit unknowns, and factuality, accessibility, and escalation checks.

## workflow-plan-explanation

Show the complete plan as a static numbered sequence.

Text alternative: The plan records instructions, trusted evidence, untrusted input, constraints, output contract, observable steps, checkpoints, budgets, recovery behavior, and stop or handoff.

## evidence-log-explanation

Display the complete log with written statuses and no animated row changes.

Text alternative: Each row records a safe artifact reference, source or tool, acceptance criterion, observable result, human decision, and timestamp.

## verification-and-recovery-explanation

Present the matrix and complete recovery sequence together as static tables.

Text alternative: Criteria receive Pass, Fail, Unknown, or Not applicable evidence. Recovery records failure, stop, diagnosis, correction, rerun, regression, rollback or escalation, and final state.

## recovery-simulation-explanation

Show permitted and prohibited examples simultaneously with text labels, not color alone.

Text alternative: Permitted simulations include removed evidence, copied-output corruption, local boundary tests, and paper timeouts. Production incidents, learner-data changes, and real messages are prohibited.

## rubric-and-handoff-explanation

Display the complete rubric, evidence links, and both thresholds as one static table.

Text alternative: Correctness, Evidence, Safety, Reproducibility, and Communication each carry twenty points. Knowledge and capstone scores must each reach eighty percent.
