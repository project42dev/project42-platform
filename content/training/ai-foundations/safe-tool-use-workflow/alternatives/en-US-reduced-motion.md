# Safe Tool-Use Workflow: reduced-motion presentation

Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

## tool-contract-explanation

Display all nine fields simultaneously with numbered labels and no animated completion.

Text alternative: The contract names purpose, validated inputs, response shapes, systems read or changed, side effects, call limits, retry behavior, exact approval, and verification or recovery.

## message-tool-demonstration

Present contract and preview side by side without animating the message being sent.

Text alternative: The preview exposes one recipient, channel, message, incident reference, idempotency key, one-use approval, and provider-state verification.

## visible-bounded-execution-explanation

Show the complete ladder with text labels and approval requirements; do not reveal levels through motion.

Text alternative: Approval increases from read-only inspection to reversible write, external action, spending, permission change, and destructive action.

## postcondition-verification-explanation

Display the complete matrix with written Verified, Uncertain, and Failed states.

Text alternative: Rows for records, files, deployments, and messages map each action to target-system evidence, forbidden effects, and secret-safe audit fields.

## partial-failure-recovery-explanation

Present every state and permitted transition as a static table rather than animated nodes.

Text alternative: Outcomes branch to complete, bounded retry, reconcile, resume, compensate, roll back, disable, or escalate based on verified state and authority.

## partial-deployment-demonstration

Show the before, stopped, and resume states side by side without animated traffic movement.

Text alternative: Artifact upload and configuration are verified. Traffic remains unchanged. Health is repaired and checked before a new exact traffic-shift approval.

## human-control-explanation

Show the full checklist at once with written Pass, Stop, and Escalate labels.

Text alternative: The checklist covers exact approval, least privilege, preview, cancellation, fail-closed validation, real postconditions, secret-safe audit, recovery, and adversarial testing.
