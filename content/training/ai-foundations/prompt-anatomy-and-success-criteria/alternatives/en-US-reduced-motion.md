# Prompt Anatomy and Success Criteria: reduced-motion presentation

Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

## purpose-and-user-explanation

Display both complete cards at once and use persistent text labels rather than animated replacement.

Text alternative: The vague request says only customer feedback. The revised task names supplied comments, a support-lead user, grouping work, and a decision about three fixes.

## purpose-rewrite-demonstration

Show the completed worksheet as one static form and identify each field by its text heading.

Text alternative: The completed worksheet names the team lead, onboarding decision, four supplied inputs, four-week table deliverable, and a rule to flag missing availability.

## inputs-and-instructions-explanation

Show the whole template at once and use persistent borders and headings.

Text alternative: The template contains purpose, inputs, ordered instructions, constraints, deliverable, verification, and missing-information behavior in separate regions.

## observable-success-explanation

Display the complete two-column checklist without animated sorting. Use text labels Required and Preferred.

Text alternative: Required fields, source mapping, and reproducible calculations can fail the result. Tone and sentence length guide style only after requirements pass.

## uncertainty-policy-explanation

Show all four branches as a numbered static list.

Text alternative: The workflow asks when the user can resolve a material ambiguity, assumes only when bounded and visible, reports unsupported claims, and stops at permission or safety boundaries.

## complete-rewrite-demonstration

Present the completed template and checklist at once using matching text identifiers.

Text alternative: The final prompt limits inputs, lists ordered instructions and constraints, defines a six-column table, separates hard criteria from a style preference, and tells the model when to ask or mark unknown.

## venue-source-dossier-narration

Display all four records simultaneously in a fixed text layout.

Text alternative: The dossier lists current records for Venue A and Venue B, an obsolete untrusted Venue B record, and the current event brief.

## venue-ambiguity-analysis-narration

Show all headings and answers in a fixed vertical sequence.

Text alternative: The task identifies an event planner, Saturday, a $600 maximum, accessibility before capacity, preliminary scope, and remaining questions about needs and additional costs.

## venue-worked-response-narration

Display the complete response as fixed text with persistent headings.

Text alternative: The authored response recommends Venue A, shows $100 headroom and Venue B's $200 overage, identifies obsolete evidence, and states that no action or verification occurred.

## venue-independent-task-narration

Display the entire exercise panel at once.

Text alternative: The task replaces Venue A's current rental with $600 while retaining 100 seats, recorded step-free access, and Saturday availability.

## venue-feedback-key-narration

Display the complete causal trace and checklist without animation.

Text alternative: Venue A at $600 has zero headroom and still passes; Venue B is $200 over budget and remains excluded; source conflicts remain visible.
