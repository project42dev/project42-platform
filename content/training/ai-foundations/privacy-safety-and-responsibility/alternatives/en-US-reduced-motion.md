Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

Text alternative: The gate asks what the data is, whether its use is authorized, which product is approved, what is retained, who can access it, whether it is necessary, and what can be removed.

Show the two text panels side by side with written reasons for every removal or retained field.

Text alternative: The original contains identity, accommodation, salary, customer detail, and a token. The revised brief keeps observable work facts, generalizes the customer, removes personal data, and excludes the secret.

## risk-follows-consequence-explanation

Present both use cases and all risk factors in a static comparison table.

Text alternative: Private ideation has low consequence and high reversibility. Applicant ranking has high consequence, wider exposure, sensitive data, fairness risks, and a need for qualified oversight and correction.

## responsible-dimensions-explanation

Display all lenses, questions, and example evidence in one static grid.

Text alternative: Privacy covers data handling; security covers unauthorized access and abuse; safety covers harm; fairness covers differing performance and impact; transparency covers disclosure and correction; accountability covers ownership.

## build-controls-explanation

Display the complete control map and example safeguards at once without animated sequencing.

Text alternative: Purpose and ownership lead to preventive controls, detection, incident response, recovery and correction, continuous monitoring, re-evaluation, and retirement.
