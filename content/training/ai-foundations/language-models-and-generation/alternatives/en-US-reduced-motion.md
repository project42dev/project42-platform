# How Language Models Produce Responses: reduced-motion presentation

Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

## welcome-and-outcomes

Show the title and all five objectives at once without animation.

Text alternative: The class covers training versus inference, token-by-token generation, model-specific tokens, evidence labels, and correction of unsupported answers.

## training-and-inference-explanation

Display both columns simultaneously and use a persistent text outline to identify the column being discussed.

Text alternative: Training uses examples to adjust model parameters. Inference uses trained parameters with a current prompt to generate a response.

## tokens-context-generation-explanation

Present the four generation steps as numbered rows without animated token movement.

Text alternative: The model receives context, estimates possible next tokens, selects one through decoding, adds it to the context, and repeats.

## inputs-shape-output-explanation
