# Agents, Tools, and Guardrails: reduced-motion presentation

Present every visual as a complete static composition. Do not make completion
depend on animation timing, autoplay, or pointer gestures.

## system-types-explanation

Display all three system types and five comparison rows simultaneously.

Text alternative: The comparison asks who controls the next step, whether tools affect systems, what state persists, whether execution loops, and how completion is decided.

## simplest-design-demonstration

Present the three designs in static columns with written benefits, risks, and required controls.

Text alternative: Model advice explains the process. A deterministic flow validates identity and issues one link. An adaptive agent is reserved for measured exceptions and cannot bypass identity controls.

## bounded-loop-explanation

Show the complete loop and every surrounding boundary in one static diagram.

Text alternative: The loop observes state, chooses an action, passes deterministic validation, uses a tool, verifies the postcondition, and stops, continues, or hands off under explicit limits.

## trajectory-evaluation-explanation

Display the complete table with written Pass, Fail, Unknown, and Not attempted statuses.

Text alternative: Each step records state, tool, target, approval, result, postcondition, cost, and the decision to continue, stop, or hand off.

## recovery-explanation

Present all checkpoint fields and recovery routes as a numbered static table.

Text alternative: The checkpoint records contract, state, approvals, results, postconditions, uncertainties, and next permitted decisions, then routes to resume, compensate, rollback, disable, or handoff.

## handoff-demonstration

Present incomplete and complete handoffs side by side without animated transfer.

Text alternative: The handoff names the goal, conflicting records, completed checks, confirmed absence of a refund, open decision, and safe case reference.

## layered-guardrails-explanation

Show every layer and its purpose in one static labeled composition.

Text alternative: Identity, minimum data, typed tools, exact action approval, budgets, audit, postcondition verification, and human handoff constrain the loop.
