# Project 42 brand concept review

**Tracking:** AB#6167–AB#6171  
**Status:** Cosmic Answer selected; production validation and implementation pending  
**Generated:** 2026-07-27

This packet contains five materially different directions for the Project 42
identity, small mark, public landing hero, and Learn and Field Guide sub-brands.
They are concepts, not approved production assets. On 2026-07-27, the owner
selected **Cosmic Answer** as the default Project 42 direction. It must now be
rebuilt as canonical SVG and semantic web assets, tested, reviewed for conflicting
marks, and accepted before replacing the current production brand.

Open [showcase.html](showcase.html) to compare the complete concepts at full size.
The first selected-direction landing explorations are in
[`selected/cosmic-answer/landing-mockups/`](selected/cosmic-answer/landing-mockups/README.md).

## Selected product promise

Brand implementation must preserve this approved promise:

> Project 42 makes artificial intelligence understandable from your first
> question to your first reliable agent. Learn at your pace, check what you
> know, and keep a record of your progress.

Cosmic language and imagery support that promise; they do not replace it.

## Directions

| 5 | Open Orbit | An open, continuously reviewed learning and contribution loop | Strongest open-source and content-freshness story | Bright palette may feel less serious in governance contexts |

## Common production requirements

- The compact mark must remain legible at 16, 24, 32, and 48 pixels.
- Every treatment needs light, dark, monochrome, and forced-colors variants.
- Text is never baked into generated hero artwork.
- Hero artwork is decorative; meaningful page content remains semantic HTML.
- Motion is optional and must honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Learn and Field Guide use a shared parent identity plus an explicit readable
  product label. Color alone never communicates which product is active.
- Provider logos, robot heads, and “AI brain” clichés remain outside the core mark.
- Production acceptance includes contrast, keyboard, zoom, reflow, screen-reader,
  favicon, social-preview, and print validation.

## Provenance

The five hero explorations were generated with OpenAI image generation from original
Project 42 prompts on 2026-07-27. Prompts explicitly prohibited text, provider marks,
watermarks, and copyrighted *Hitchhiker's Guide* imagery. The compact SVG concept
marks were authored for this review packet and use system fonts only.

Generated artwork is ideation evidence. It is not automatically publishable.
Human owner selection, originality review, accessibility review, and production
reconstruction are required.
