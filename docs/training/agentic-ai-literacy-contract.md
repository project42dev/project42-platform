# Agentic-AI literacy delivery contract

Project 42 teaches agentic AI as observable system behavior, not as a provider
ranking or a marketing label. The machine-readable contract is
`content/training/agentic-ai-literacy/delivery-contract.json`. It validates
against
`schemas/training/agentic-ai-literacy-contract.schema.json` and consumes the
dated evidence matrix in
`content/reference/agentic-ai-product-evidence.json`.

## One taxonomy across Learn and Field Guide

Learn and Field Guide must use the same eight classes:

1. model;
2. chatbot;
3. assistant;
4. copilot;
5. deterministic workflow;
6. agent;
7. agentic system; and
8. multi-agent system.

The unit of classification is an exact, version-scoped product experience. A
provider family can contain models, assistants, frameworks, developer tools, and
managed agent services with different classifications. A tool-call proposal is
not evidence that the model executed a tool. Multiple model calls are not
evidence of multiple agents. Marketing words are discovery clues, not proof.

## Learning progression

The five-stage sequence moves from plain-language separation to defensible
system evidence:

1. Beginners name the model, product, and runtime layers.
2. Beginners trace whether code or a model chooses the next executable step.
3. Practitioners locate tools, state, side effects, verification, recovery, and
   human authority.
4. Practitioners compare exact product experiences across provider families
   using dated evidence.
5. Advanced learners defend deterministic, single-agent, agentic, and
   multi-agent classifications with run and handoff evidence.

Each stage produces retained mastery evidence. A learner does not pass merely by
recognizing a definition. They must classify behavior, show the boundary, cite
the evidence, preserve unknowns, and identify the accountable human.

## Field Guide delivery

The Field Guide receives five connected assets:

- a one-minute decision card;
- an eight-class comparison table;
- dated provider-experience case cards;
- a claim-verification checklist; and
- a plain-language terms and misconceptions guide.

Every asset deep-links to the relevant Learn stage and derives volatile product
claims from the same evidence matrix. The Field Guide must not maintain a second
copy of provider facts that can drift independently.

## Assessment and mastery

The delivery requires at least five formative checks and eight product cases.
Learners compare the same behavior across alternatives, receive corrective
feedback, may reattempt, and retain attempt history.

An 80 percent knowledge score is necessary but insufficient. Critical mistakes
must be absent from the mastery submission: provider-wide generalization,
marketing labels used as proof, treating a tool-call response as an agent,
collapsing the model and harness, hiding unknowns, omitting human authority, or
making unsupported autonomy claims. A person reviews the applied classification
dossier before mastery is awarded.

## Accessibility and virtual-instructor readiness

Every substantive lesson requires a complete narration script, captions,
transcript, text-only alternative, reduced-motion alternative, non-color
classification cues, and an explicit assessment handoff. Visual control-flow
examples must remain understandable as ordered text and may not communicate a
classification only through color, motion, position, or an icon.

These are delivery requirements for the content packages. They do not depend on
the deferred virtual-instructor player runtime.

## Freshness and publication

Product examples use first-party sources with a maximum 90-day review cadence.
Every claim remains labeled as documented, tested, inference, or unknown. A
documentation statement does not become tested evidence, and a stale case blocks
publication until it is reverified or downgraded.

Generated changes remain draft. Editorial, subject-matter, accessibility,
safety, and publication approvals are all required. No model, agent, provider,
or automated maintenance workflow can approve its own content for publication.
