## What this draft originally proposed

Draft 0.1, prepared 2026-07-27, was plain-language proposed copy for the
canonical `project-42.dev/legal` page, covering: who created and operates
Project 42; how AI contributes to content, including the no-autonomous-publish
rule; the Apache 2.0 and CC BY 4.0 license split and its limits; the
educational-information and no-professional-advice framing; service, account,
and learner-record availability and recovery language; a pointer to privacy and
consent controls rather than restating them; third-party service disclaimers;
placeholder contact fields the owner had not yet filled in; and a required
review record listing six items to close before publication.

Every one of those topics is now covered by the shipped page, which additionally
adds Acceptable use, Warranty and liability, and a Version and sources section
this draft never reached. Where the two differ, the shipped page is correct and
this draft is superseded, not the other way around.
